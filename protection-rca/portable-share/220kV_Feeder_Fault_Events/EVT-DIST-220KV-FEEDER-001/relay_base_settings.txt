--- PRIMARY-SIDE DATA CHANNELS -----------------------------------------
COMTRADE records include BOTH secondary (as-measured by relay, CT/VT
output) and primary-referred (actual line-side kA/kV) analog channels:
  Secondary : IA, IB, IC, IN (Amps)   VA, VB, VC (Volts)
  Primary   : IA_PRI, IB_PRI, IC_PRI, IN_PRI (kA)   VA_PRI, VB_PRI, VC_PRI (kV)
Primary channels = secondary channel x CT_RATIO or VT_RATIO / 1000.
